# 90-second demo video script

Screen-record the app (browser at 100% zoom, sidebar open). Rehearse once; keep the cursor slow.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Title slide, then the app home page | "Factories have plenty of data. What they lack is knowing what matters and what to do next. This is Maintenance Copilot, built on AMD Token Factory." |
| 0:10–0:25 | Point at the headline metrics and the blue info box | "Our demo plant has five machines. The copilot flagged the M3 pump 39 hours before a normal fixed vibration alarm would fire. At five thousand dollars an hour of downtime, that is about one hundred ninety-five thousand dollars avoidable." |
| 0:25–0:40 | Fleet & alerts tab: table, then the vibration chart with the fixed-alarm line | "Only M3-Pump is flagged. Here the vibration trend drifts away from baseline days before it crosses the fixed alarm line." |
| 0:40–1:05 | AI diagnosis tab → click **Diagnose with AMD**; cards appear; expand Sources | "One click sends the sensor evidence plus the most relevant manual sections and past work orders to Gemma 4 31B running on AMD Token Factory. It returns a cause, urgency, a concrete action, a safety note, and the exact sources. Confidence is shown, and when the model is unsure or urgency is high, the app asks for human review." |
| 1:05–1:20 | Ask tab: type "How do I replace the drive-end bearing safely?" | "Technicians can also ask questions. Answers come only from the plant's manual and log, with sources." |
| 1:20–1:30 | Sidebar AMD panel, then change $/hour and show the headline updating | "The sidebar shows the AMD endpoint, model, latency and tokens for every call: a diagnosis in under ten seconds. Earlier warnings, planned repairs, less downtime. Thank you." |

Tips: record at 1080p, narrate in one take, keep total under 90 seconds.
