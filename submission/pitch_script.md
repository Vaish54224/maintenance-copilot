# 90-second demo video script

Setup: browser at 1920×1080, 100% zoom, sidebar open. Open the app once before recording (first load takes a few seconds) and run one diagnosis to warm the AMD endpoint. Turn on **Demo mode** in the sidebar as a safety net: if a live call fails on camera, it offers a cached answer that is clearly labelled "not live" (say so if you use it).

The whole demo is 3 clicks: **Diagnose with AMD**, **one suggested chat question**, **change $/hour**.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Home view: hero, KPI strip, fleet cards, trend chart (everything fits in one screen) | "Factories have plenty of sensor data. What they lack is knowing what matters and what to do next. This is Maintenance Copilot, running on AMD." |
| 0:10–0:25 | Point at the big teal **$195,000** KPI and **+39 h** next to it | "On our demo plant, the copilot flagged the M3 pump 39 hours before a normal fixed vibration alarm would fire. At five thousand dollars an hour of downtime, that is about one hundred ninety-five thousand dollars avoidable." |
| 0:25–0:40 | Fleet cards (M3-Pump glowing red), then the chart: teal "AI flags drift" line, red "Fixed alarm fires" line, "+39 h earlier" bracket | "Five machines, one in trouble. Each is compared with its own seven-day baseline. The teal line is where we flagged the drift; the red line is where a fixed alarm finally fires. That gap is the planning time we give back." |
| 0:40–1:05 | **AI diagnosis** tab → click **Diagnose with AMD**; staged progress, then the result cards; hover the safety callout and open one source chip | "One click sends the sensor evidence plus the most relevant manual sections and past work orders to Gemma 4 31B on AMD Token Factory. Back comes a cause, urgency, confidence, numbered actions, a safety callout, and the exact sources. High urgency, so the copilot asks a human to confirm before acting." |
| 1:05–1:20 | **Technician chat** tab → click the chip "How do I replace the drive-end bearing safely?"; answer with source chips | "Technicians can ask questions too. Answers come only from the plant's own manual and log, with the sources right underneath." |
| 1:20–1:30 | Sidebar: change downtime cost (e.g. 5,000 → 10,000) and the headline doubles; point at the AMD status card (model, latency, tokens) | "Change the cost of downtime and the business case updates live. Every call's latency and tokens are shown here. Earlier warnings, planned repairs, less downtime. Thank you." |

Tips: record at 1080p, narrate in one take, keep total under 90 seconds. Typical live diagnosis takes 6–20 s on the shared key; cut the wait in editing if needed.
